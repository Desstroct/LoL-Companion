How it works

Combines counter strength vs your lane opponent with ally synergy to suggest the best picks for your team.

⚔️ vs [enemy] — Lane opponent identified; picks scored against them + your allies.
★ vs N enemies — No lane opponent yet; scored against all locked enemies + allies.

Picks update automatically as allies and enemies lock in.

Controls:
• Rotate dial to scroll through suggestions
• Press key/dial or touch to refresh

Data from Lolalytics.
