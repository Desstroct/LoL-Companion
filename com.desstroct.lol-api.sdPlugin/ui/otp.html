How it works

Other actions (Auto Rune, Best Item) check your OTP list to apply per-champion settings.

Lane: Override the lane used for data lookups — useful when autofilled.
Auto Rune: Uncheck to disable auto rune for this champion only.
Auto Spell: Uncheck to disable auto spell for this champion only.

Key / Dial: Browse your OTP list. During Champion Select the dial auto-jumps to your current champion and shows ACTIVE.
