How it works

During Champion Select, fetches the optimal rune page from Lolalytics.

Auto mode: Runes applied automatically when you lock in. Key border turns green on success.
Manual mode: Press the key/dial to apply runes when ready.

Dial rotate: Switch between "Highest WR" and "Most Popular" builds.
Auto Spells: Also sets the highest win-rate summoner spell combo.
Matchup: Adapts runes to the enemy laner. Falls back to generic if <50 games of data.
